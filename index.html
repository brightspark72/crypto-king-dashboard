Action plan

General framework · not financial advice
  1. Stop the bleed first. No new buys and no averaging down into any losing thesis until the book is cleaned up.
  2. Recycle dead trades. Anything that fails the rules (not liquid on Binance Spot, broken thesis, exploit, delisting/Monitoring Tag, heavy unlock overhang) gets sold, ideally into green days or relief bounces, and parked in USDT.
  3. Correlated names = one risk. Keep the single best name per narrative (e.g. one L2, one L1 alt) and recycle the rest.
  4. Fix concentration. No single alt above ~15% of the book; trim oversized winners into strength rather than all at once.
  5. Rebuild the core. Target roughly BTC 30–35%, ETH 20–25%, stables 15–20% dry powder, and 3–4 liquid narrative names at 25–30% combined.
  6. Redeploy only on rules. Tokenomics checked first (FDV/MC, unlocks, emissions), Binance Spot liquidity confirmed, spot only, no leverage, staged entries.
  7. Dust. Positions under ~£5 are not worth fees or time; use Binance "Convert small balances" where supported or ignore them.
  8. UK tax: worth checking. Realised losses can generally offset gains for Capital Gains Tax if reported to HMRC. Keep records of every disposal and check the rules (or ask an adviser) before year-end. This is not tax advice.
